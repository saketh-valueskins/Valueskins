## PAGE 2: HOW IT WORKS (Fund Flow & Monetization)

**Heading:** How ValueSkins Works

**Subheading:** Secure, transparent, documented — from start to finish

### Section 1: The Deal Lifecycle

**Step 1: Discovery**
Brands post campaign briefs or creators pitch to brands. Both can search and filter by profession, experience, rates, and past work.

**Step 2: Negotiation**
Direct messaging between creator and brand to finalize terms, deliverables, timeline, and budget. Everything is documented in the deal.

**Step 3: Funding**
Brand transfers agreed amount to ValueSkins escrow via Razorpay (INR) or Stripe (USD). Creator is notified. Work period begins.

**Step 4: Delivery**
Creator delivers work within the agreed timeline. All deliverables are documented in the platform.

**Step 5: Review & Approval**
Brand reviews work. If approved, they mark deal as complete. If changes needed, creator gets 48 hours to revise.

**Step 6: Payment Release**
Once approved, ValueSkins releases payment to creator's bank account within 2-3 business days. Platform fee (2%) is deducted automatically.

### Section 2: What Happens If Things Go Wrong

**Creator Doesn't Deliver:**
If creator doesn't deliver within agreed timeline, brand can request automatic refund. Refund is processed within 24 hours to brand's original payment method.

**Brand Doesn't Approve:**
If brand rejects work, creator has 48 hours to revise. If revision submitted, brand reviews again. If rejected twice, ValueSkins team reviews and decides.

**Disputed Deliverables:**
If either party disputes the work quality or timeline, ValueSkins team reviews all documented evidence (messages, deliverables, timelines) and makes final decision within 7 days.

### Section 3: Commission & Fees

- **ValueSkins Platform Fee:** 2% of deal value (deducted from creator payout)
- **Payment Processing Fee:** Included in platform fee (no additional cost)
- **Refund:** No fee charged for cancelled/refunded deals
- **Appeal/Dispute Resolution:** No fee, handled by our team

**Example:** Brand funds ₹10,000 deal
- Creator delivers, brand approves
- ValueSkins fee: ₹200
- Creator receives: ₹9,800
- ValueSkins receives: ₹200

### Section 4: Payout Schedule

- Payouts happen every Monday
- Minimum payout threshold: ₹500
- Processing time: 2-3 business days from payout date
- Bank transfer direct to creator's registered account

### Section 5: Fund Safety

All brand payments are held in escrow by regulated payment partners:
- **Razorpay** (for INR transactions) — RBI regulated, ISO 27001 certified
- **Stripe** (for USD transactions) — PCI-DSS Level 1, SOC 2 compliant

Creator funds are never at risk. Payment only releases after explicit brand approval.

## PAGE 3: REFUND & DISPUTE POLICY

**Heading:** Refund, Cancellation & Dispute Resolution

**Subheading:** Clear timelines, fair processes, documented decisions

### Section 1: Deal Cancellation

**Before Payment Funded:**
Either party can cancel without penalty. Deal is removed, no charges.

**After Payment Funded (Before Work Starts):**
- Within first 48 hours: Brand can request full refund (less 2% platform fee already deducted from escrow)
- After 48 hours: Automatic refund issued unless creator has started work

**Creator Didn't Start Work:**
If creator doesn't mark work as started within 72 hours of payment, brand can auto-cancel and receive full refund within 24 hours.

### Section 2: Refund Process

**Refund Requests:**
All refund requests must be filed through the platform with a reason (e.g., "Creator unresponsive", "Budget constraints", "Scope changed")

**Processing Timeline:**
- Instant refunds (before work started): Processed within 24 hours
- Disputed refunds: Investigated within 7 days, decision provided within 14 days

**Refund Method:**
- Original payment method (same card/account used)
- For Razorpay: Same bank account, 2-3 business days
- For Stripe: Same payment method, 3-5 business days

**No Refund Scenarios:**
- Work completed and approved by brand
- Work delivered and brand requested minor revisions (creator has 48h to revise)
- Deal marked complete by both parties

### Section 3: Dispute Resolution

**Minor Disputes (Work Quality):**
1. Creator has 48 hours to revise after brand feedback
2. Brand reviews revision
3. If brand still unsatisfied, ValueSkins team reviews
4. Team decision within 7 days (hold payment in escrow during review)

**Timeline Disputes (Missed Deadlines):**
1. If creator missed agreed deadline, brand can mark deal as failed
2. Creator has 24 hours to explain or provide extension request
3. ValueSkins reviews documented timeline and extension request
4. Decision within 7 days

**Scope Disputes (Deliverables Don't Match Agreement):**
1. Brand documents what was agreed vs. what was delivered
2. Creator responds with evidence of what was agreed
3. ValueSkins reviews all messages, deal terms, and deliverables
4. Team decision within 7 days

**Payment Disputes (Duplicate Charges, Wrong Amount):**
1. File dispute with reason and evidence
2. ValueSkins team investigates within 24 hours
3. If duplicate or error confirmed, refund issued within 2 business days
4. If not duplicate, decision explained to filer

### Section 4: Appeal Process

If you disagree with a ValueSkins decision:
1. Submit appeal within 14 days with new evidence
2. Independent team member reviews (not original decision maker)
3. Decision within 7 days
4. Appeal decision is final

### Section 5: Escalation for Serious Issues

If a creator or brand violates serious terms (fraud, harassment, non-delivery, etc.):
1. Issue is flagged by the other party or auto-detected by system
2. ValueSkins team conducts full investigation (3-7 days)
3. If confirmed:
   - Refund issued to brand immediately
   - Account suspended pending further review
   - Permanent ban if repeat violations
4. User has right to appeal within 14 days

## PAGE 4: CREATOR VERIFICATION & FRAUD PREVENTION

**Heading:** How We Keep ValueSkins Safe

**Subheading:** Verification, fraud detection, and community protection

### Section 1: Creator Verification

All creators must complete verification to receive payments:

**Identity Verification:**
- Valid government ID (Aadhar, Passport, Driving License)
- Government-issued ID must match profile name
- Photos are compared for authenticity
- Takes 1-2 business days

**Bank Account Verification:**
- Bank account must match creator's legal name
- Micro-deposit verification (₹1 sent, creator confirms amount)
- Account confirmed before first payout
- Protects against money laundering

**Portfolio Review (For Professional Profiles):**
- Creators must provide examples of past work
- If portfolio shows relevant experience, verified badge is awarded
- Manual review for high-value deals (₹50K+)

**Age Verification:**
- All users must be 18+ to transact
- Age verified against government ID
- Underage accounts are immediately suspended
- Protects against child labor and exploitation

### Section 2: Brand Verification

Brands complete similar verification to ensure legitimacy:

**Company Registration:**
- Brand must provide company registration proof
- Company name must match on platform
- Legal business address verified

**Tax ID Verification:**
- GST registration (for India) or business license required
- Used to verify legitimate business operations

**Payment Account Verification:**
- Brand's payment method must match company registration
- Credit card cardholder name matched against company details

### Section 3: Fraud Detection & Prevention

**Transaction Monitoring:**
- All deals monitored for suspicious patterns
- Multiple rapid payments to same creator = flagged
- Unusually high deal values auto-reviewed
- Cross-platform activity monitored

**Behavior Analysis:**
- Creator suddenly accepting high-value deals after low activity = flagged
- Brand posting deals, immediately cancelling, then reposting = flagged
- Rapid account creation + large deal = auto-suspended pending review
- Communication patterns monitored for scams

**Machine Learning Fraud Detection:**
- System learns patterns of legitimate vs. fraudulent behavior
- New accounts monitored more closely (first 5 deals)
- Anomalous activity triggers manual review within 24 hours

### Section 4: Chargeback & Refund Fraud Prevention

**Brand Chargeback Prevention:**
- Brands funding deals creates documented transaction record
- All communication is logged and timestamped
- If brand disputes with their bank, we submit full evidence
- Proven delivery reduces chargeback success rate to near zero

**Refund Abuse Prevention:**
- Brands attempting multiple refund requests = flagged
- Pattern of requesting refunds after delivery = account restriction
- Repeated offenders = permanent ban

### Section 5: Community Reporting

Users can report:
- Suspicious profiles
- Fraudulent activity
- Harassment or abuse
- Non-delivery or scam attempts

All reports reviewed within 24 hours. Confirmed violations result in:
- Account suspension (pending investigation)
- Refund to affected party
- Permanent ban for confirmed fraud or serious violations
- Information shared with payment processors for wider bans

### Section 6: Payment Processor Security

ValueSkins uses regulated payment partners:
- Razorpay: RBI regulated, ISO 27001 certified, PCI-DSS Level 1
- Stripe: SOC 2 Type II certified, PCI-DSS Level 1, ISO 27001

All transactions encrypted end-to-end. Funds held in isolated escrow accounts. No ValueSkins employees can access customer funds.

## PAGE 5: DATA SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

**Heading:** Data Security & Compliance

**Subheading:** Your data is protected. Here's how.

### Section 1: Encryption

**Data in Transit:**
- All data encrypted with TLS 1.3 (latest standard)
- HTTPS enforced on all pages (no unencrypted HTTP)
- API endpoints use TLS 1.3 with strong ciphers

**Data at Rest:**
- Sensitive data encrypted with AES-256-GCM
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds, salted, never plaintext)
- Payment information tokenized and encrypted
- Encryption keys stored in AWS KMS (separate from application)

### Section 2: Access Control

**Role-Based Access:**
- Only authorized employees can access customer data
- Access logs maintained for all data reads
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for admin access
- Annual security training mandatory for all staff

**Database Security:**
- Row-level security (RLS) enforced on all tables
- Users only see their own data
- Admin access requires 2-person approval
- Database backups encrypted separately

### Section 3: Backup & Recovery

**Daily Automated Backups:**
- Automatic backup every 24 hours
- Cross-region replication (different AWS regions)
- Encryption maintained in backups (AES-256)
- Backup retention: 90 days

**Disaster Recovery:**
- Monthly restore testing to ensure backups work
- Recovery time objective: <1 hour
- Tested procedures for data loss scenarios

### Section 4: Regulatory Compliance

**India:**

### Section 5: Third-Party Vendors

All vendors vetted for security:
